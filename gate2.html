CabbageSuper · experiments 002–003 · cross-leaf transfer and abstention

Will the second leaf obey?

The donor leaf teaches us which fine-residue patches belong to which coarse neighborhoods. An additional donor-calibrated confidence gate may refuse to synthesize at unfamiliar addresses. The target leaf supplies only its coarse geometry during synthesis. Its hidden fine detail is revealed only to score the reconstruction. We also test an independent texture, a deliberately wrong parent, zero detail, and the exact same generated Fourier power with randomized phase.

What this experiment cannot yet claim

The model retrieves existing 9×9 patches using handcrafted coarse descriptors. It has not learned an arbitrary neural operator or the biological rules of leaf venation. Target coarse geometry is supplied from the real target; therefore the output is conditional detail synthesis, not complete leaf creation. Power-spectrum equality is only between the conditioned result and its phase-scrambled counterpart, not between all the baselines. The *ungated* model fails the flat-field negative control by hallucinating detail. Gate 3 implements a donor-only 95th-percentile nearest-neighbor confidence threshold; it refuses the flat target and reduces errors on the unrelated waves in the synthetic controls. Whether that rule improves natural leaves is entirely untested, and its rejection regions are visualized rather than hidden.
